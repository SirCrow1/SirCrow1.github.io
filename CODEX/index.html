Install in four steps

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere permanent.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions / brave://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open a chat, click the C.O.D.E.X icon, tick your messages, and press PDF or Word.

Long chats load in pieces. Scroll to the top first, then press Rescan in the popup.

Privacy

It doesn't look at your chats—it just converts them into PDF and Word files locally on your computer.
