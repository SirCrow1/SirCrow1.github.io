📖 World Glossary & Power Systems

Spirit Energy

An innate spiritual force bound to an individual's soul, manifesting through nature elements like Shadow, Flame, Water, Wind, Earth, and Light.

Astro Energy

A mysterious cosmic energy reserve that acts as a multiplier to combat mastery and physical endurance. Rare individuals possess cosmic affinity.
